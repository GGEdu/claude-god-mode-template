
## Dominio: Supply Chain

### Áreas cubiertas
- **Carrier management**: scorecards, RFP, rate negotiation
- **Inventory**: demand forecasting, safety stock, ABC/XYZ classification
- **Logistics exceptions**: escalation tiers, claims, root cause analysis
- **Customs/trade**: HS classification, Incoterms, FTA utilization
- **Production**: scheduling, changeover optimization, bottleneck resolution
- **Quality**: NCR lifecycle, CAPA, SPC interpretation
- **Returns**: RMA, grading, disposition, vendor recovery
- **Energy procurement**: tariffs, PPAs, demand charges
